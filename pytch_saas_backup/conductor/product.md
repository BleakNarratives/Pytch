# Product Definition

## Vision
To create a revenue-generating SaaS platform that combines autonomous B2B lead generation (Pytch) with advanced process optimization (Process Miner). The goal is to provide a "business-in-a-box" solution that not only finds leads but optimizes the sales and delivery process, delivering immediate value to users to secure subscription revenue.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Automated Revenue Generation:** Users can set up campaigns and let the AI handle lead sourcing and outreach.
- **Process Optimization:** Integrated analytics to refine sales pipelines and operational workflows.
- **Accessible SaaS:** A web-based platform usable by non-technical business owners, available immediately for subscription.

## Target Audience
- **Freelancers & Agencies:** Who need consistent deal flow but lack time for manual outreach.
- **Small Business Owners:** Who need to optimize operations and sales without hiring a large team.
- **Sales Teams:** Looking for automated tools to increase efficiency.

## Key Features
1.  **Pytch Lead Engine (Core):**
    -   AI-powered cold email generation (Claude Sonnet integration).
    -   Automated sending and follow-up sequences.
    -   Campaign performance tracking (Open rates, Reply rates).
2.  **Process Miner Integration (Secondary):**
    -   Analyze sales pipeline bottlenecks.
    -   Optimize email timing and sequence based on response data.
3.  **SaaS Infrastructure:**
    -   User Authentication & Subscription Management (Stripe integration).
    -   Multi-tenant database architecture.
    -   Web Dashboard for campaign management and analytics.

## Revenue Model
- **Tiered Subscription:**
    -   **Starter:** Limited emails/leads per month.
    -   **Pro:** Higher limits, advanced process analytics.
    -   **Enterprise:** Custom integrations and volume.
