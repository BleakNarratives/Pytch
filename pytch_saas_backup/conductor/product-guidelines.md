# Product Guidelines

## Brand Voice & Tone
- **Primary:** **Witty & Bold.** We are not just another boring tool. We are opinionated about how business should be done (automated, efficient, smart). We use sharp, memorable copy that stands out.
- **Secondary:** **Hyper-Professional (Undertone).** While we are witty, we are dead serious about results. The wit never obscures clarity. When discussing money, security, or data, we shift to a precise, confident competence.
- **Motto:** "Make Money, Not Busywork."

## Visual Identity
- **Aesthetic:** High-contrast, clean, modern SaaS. Think dark mode defaults with vibrant accent colors (Neon Green or Electric Blue) that signal "active" and "profit".
- **UI Density:** Information-rich but not cluttered. Dashboards should look like a cockpit—powerful controls, clear metrics.

## UX Principles
- **Speed to Value:** Users must reach their "Aha!" moment (launching a campaign) within 5 minutes of sign-up.
- **Radical Transparency:** No hidden fees, no confusing metrics. If a campaign is failing, tell them why plainly.
- **The "Mint" Factor:** Every session should end with a small delight—a satisfying animation when a deal closes, a witty confirmation message, or a proactive insight that saves them time.

## Writing Style
- **Headlines:** Punchy, verb-driven, benefit-focused.
- **Body Copy:** Concise, scannable. Avoid jargon unless it's industry-standard.
- **Error Messages:** Helpful, human, and slightly apologetic (without groveling). Explain *how* to fix it.

## Coding Standards (Frontend)
- **Components:** Modular, reusable, and strictly typed.
- **State Management:** Predictable and traceable.
- **Performance:** Sub-100ms interaction latency.
